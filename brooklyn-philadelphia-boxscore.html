Methodology & QA

Both series cover January 2020 through September 2026 with 81 unique month-start dates, no missing prices, no missing months, no nonpositive values, and no duplicate dates. Five models were evaluated over the same 44 expanding-window one-month-ahead forecasts; each city selected the model with the lowest mean absolute error. The initial training window contains 37 observations; validation targets run from February 2023 through September 2026. Selection and error reporting share these validation windows, so these results are not an independent post-selection test. Models are re-fitted to all available data to forecast October 2026.

Interpretation: Brooklyn leads in projected home value; Philadelphia leads in the scale-adjusted MAE proxy. Relative MAE here is backtest MAE divided by the latest observed market value, not MAPE. No causal inference or investment advice is implied.
