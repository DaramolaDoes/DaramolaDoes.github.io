How standings are ranked

Rank is based only on forecasted price, highest to lowest. Historical average, high and low describe the observed Zillow ZHVI condo/co-op series and do not affect rank.

Automatic weekly update

The page reads finalized matchups from schedule-data.json. When a matchup is marked final and its four historical fields are added, both city studies appear automatically and the ranking is recalculated.

Historical values are derived from Zillow Home Value Index condo/co-op series selected by geography and property type. ChrionML® independently compiles, quality-checks, models and interprets the data. Forecasts are analytical estimates—not appraisals, investment advice or guarantees.
