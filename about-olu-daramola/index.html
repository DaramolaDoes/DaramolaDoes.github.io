Founder and research lead

Olu (Tim) Daramola.

Olu (Tim) Daramola founded ChrionML® AI Labs to make applied machine-learning research inspectable, understandable and useful—connecting rigorous quantitative methods with production systems and real-world decisions.

Applied machine learningQuantitative researchMLOps and platformsResearch communication

Research leadership

Olu leads the ChrionML® 64-City Intelligence program, including research design, source qualification, dataset construction, quality assurance, Python modeling, chronological backtesting, interpretation and publication. The program tests a repeatable research standard across heterogeneous local markets without forcing a single model to win everywhere.

His broader work spans artificial intelligence, machine-learning products, research platforms and data-driven decision systems across healthcare, biotechnology, financial markets and technology.

Research objective

ChrionML®’s goal is to create a cumulative public record of applied model evaluation: source-linked data, explicit assumptions, comparable measures, interpretable findings and documented limitations. Each project is written for technical practitioners and decision-makers who need to understand both the result and how it was produced.
